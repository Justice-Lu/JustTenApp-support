Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Just Ten respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

Nothing.

Just Ten is a fully offline puzzle game. It collects no personal data, analytics, location information, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

Data stored on your device

The app stores local game progress on your device only:

  • Your high score and best wave.
  • Your lifetime career stats, including games finished, waves completed, tiles cleared, largest selection, and fastest wave.
  • Your sound, haptics, Newbie mode, and theme settings.
  • The achievements you have earned.
  • A timestamp used to throttle Apple's in-app rating prompt.

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Third-party services

Just Ten includes no third-party analytics, advertising, SDKs, or crash reporting. It makes no network requests.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to just.develope.acc@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new last-updated date.
