Just Ten

Support

Just Ten is a fully offline puzzle game. No account or internet connection is required.

Quick help

  • Drag a rectangle over two or more number tiles that add up to exactly 10.
  • Clear all available moves to advance through ten waves.
  • Sound, haptics, Newbie mode, and themes are available from Settings.
  • If the app becomes unresponsive, close it and reopen it.

Contact support

Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include passwords or sensitive information.
